1. Agent人生 是什么

Agent人生（AGENT人生）是 BNB 智能链上的 meme 代币，采用链上公平铸造，仅对持有 .ai Agent ID 的币安 Agentic Wallet 开放。机制参考 $JAGENT（jager.live）：代币只在份额被铸造时产生，无预售、无私募、无团队预留。项目由社区驱动，与币安官方无隶属关系。

2. 四大核心设计

100% 进 LP每一笔铸造资金全部进入流动性底池，不抽成、不进金库。
1% 税费做营销买入 / 卖出各收取 1% 税费，全部用于社区营销与生态增长。
LP 永久锁仓流动性代币自动锁仓至合约地址，永久锁定，任何人都无法撤出。
零提取权限合约不包含任何提款 / owner 函数，开发团队无法动用池内资产。

3. 代币

  • 名称 / 符号：Agent人生 / AGENT人生，18 位小数，BEP-20 标准。
  • 总供应量仅在铸造时产生，硬上限由代币合约强制执行；只有铸造合约可以增发。
  • 买入税 1%、卖出税 1%，税率写死在合约代码中，全部用于社区营销，不进入任何中心化金库。
  • 开盘前 AGENT人生 不可转账；达到约定铸造进度后自动开池，并在同一笔交易中解除转账锁定。

4. 铸造

  • 资格：发起交易的钱包必须持有 .ai Agent ID（链上读取 SPACE ID 注册表）。
  • 额度：每个 Agent ID 10 份；每笔交易最多 10 份。
  • 支付：用 USDT（先 approve，再带最高成本 mint）即可；也可使用 BNB 在同一笔交易内兑换为 USDT 后铸造。
  • 铸造资金 100% 进入 LP 底池，LP 代币锁仓至合约、永久锁定。

5. 流动性与税费

  • 每次铸造的 100% 资金注入官方 AGENT人生/USDT 流动性池，对应 LP 代币发往合约地址锁仓，永久无法移除。
  • 买卖 1% 税费全部用于社区营销与生态回购，费率与接收方写死在代码中、不可更改。
  • 合约不含任何 withdraw / owner 类函数，团队无权限提取池内资金，资金安全由代码保证。

6. 独立声明

Agent人生 是独立社区项目，与币安、SPACE ID、PancakeSwap 等均无关联，也未获其背书，仅使用其公开链上合约与标准接口。

1. What Agent人生 is

Agent人生 (AGENT人生) is a meme token on BNB Smart Chain with a fair, on-chain mint reserved for Binance Agentic Wallets holding a .ai Agent ID. It follows the $JAGENT (jager.live) mint mechanism: supply only comes into existence when shares are minted — no presale, no private round, no team allocation. A community-driven project, independent of Binance.

2. Four Core Principles

100% to LPEvery mint goes entirely into the liquidity pool — no skim, no treasury cut.
1% tax for marketingA 1% buy/sell tax, fully used for community marketing and ecosystem growth.
LP locked foreverLiquidity tokens are auto-locked into the contract, permanently — nobody can remove them.
Zero withdrawal powerThe contract has no withdraw / owner functions; the team cannot touch pooled funds.

3. Token

  • Name / symbol: Agent人生 / AGENT人生, 18 decimals, BEP-20.
  • Supply is created only by minting; the hard cap is enforced by the token contract. Only the mint contract can issue tokens.
  • Buy tax 1% and sell tax 1%, fixed in code, fully used for community marketing — no centralized treasury.
  • AGENT人生 cannot be transferred before the pool opens; the pool opens automatically and the transfer lock is lifted in the same transaction at the agreed mint milestone.

4. Minting

  • Eligibility: the sending wallet must hold a .ai Agent ID (read on-chain from the SPACE ID registry).
  • Quota: 10 shares per Agent ID; max 10 shares per transaction.
  • Pay with USDT (approve, then mint with a maximum cost) or with BNB swapped to USDT in the same transaction.
  • 100% of every mint goes into the LP pool; LP tokens are locked into the contract forever.

5. Liquidity & Tax

  • 100% of each mint is added to the official AGENT人生/USDT pool; the LP tokens are sent to the contract and locked permanently.
  • The 1% buy/sell tax is fully used for community marketing and ecosystem buybacks; rate and recipient are fixed in code and cannot be changed.
  • The contract contains no withdraw / owner functions, so the team has no authority to extract pooled funds — safety is guaranteed by code.

6. Independence

Agent人生 is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Binance, SPACE ID, PancakeSwap, or any other party; it only uses their public on-chain contracts and standard interfaces.
